Map Guide

Resource Nodes

  • Gold Mines - Located in center and corners
  • Forests - Scattered around the map for wood
  • Berry Bushes - Early food source
  • Deer - Hunt for food, move in herds
  • Stone Quarries - Late game stone source

Strategic Positions

  • High Ground - Defensive advantage
  • Chokepoints - Easy to defend narrow passages
  • Water Crossings - Control bridges for map control
  • Center - Most contested area for resources

Map Control Tips

  • Secure gold mines early
  • Place towers at chokepoints
  • Use scouts to deny enemy vision
  • Control water routes for mobility
